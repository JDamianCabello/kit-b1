Comparativos y superlativos

Más que, el más: depende de lo largo que sea el adjetivo.

more bigger
bigger

1Comparativo

Cortos (1 sílaba)

  • + -er than: tall → taller than
  • CVC: big → bigger
  • -y: easy → easier, happy → happier

Largos (2+ sílabas)

  • more ... than
  • more expensive than
  • more interesting than

Irregulares

  • good → better
  • bad → worse
  • far → further

2Superlativo

Cortos (1 sílaba)

  • the -est: the tallest
  • the biggest
  • the easiest

Largos (2+ sílabas)

  • the most ...
  • the most expensive
  • the most interesting

Irregulares

  • the best
  • the worst
  • the furthest

3Ejemplos

Cortos (1 sílaba)

  • My brother is taller than me.

Largos (2+ sílabas)

  • This phone is more expensive than that one.

Irregulares

  • It's the best film I've ever seen.

4as ... as (igual de)

She's as tall as me.

It's not as cold as yesterday.
