Contables e incontables

Some, any, much, many, a few, a little: la clave es si se puede contar.

an advice
some advice

1Qué son

Contables

  • Se pueden contar y tienen plural
  • an apple, two apples
  • a chair, three chairs

Incontables

  • No se cuentan, no tienen plural
  • water, money, time, bread
  • information, advice, furniture, music

2Cantidad

Contables

  • many: How many apples?
  • a few: a few friends (unos pocos)

Incontables

  • much: How much money?
  • a little: a little sugar (un poco)

3Con los dos

Contables

some (afirmativas y ofrecimientos)any (negativas y preguntas)a lot ofno

Incontables

some waterany money?a lot of timeno milk

4Some y any

  • There are some eggs in the fridge.
  • There isn't any bread.
  • Have you got any brothers?
  • Would you like some coffee? (ofrecimiento: some)
