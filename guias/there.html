There is / there are y preposiciones de lugar

«Hay» en inglés, y cómo decir dónde está cada cosa.

There is two cats.
There are two cats.

1Forma

There is (singular / incontable)

  • There's a bank near here.
  • There isn't any milk.
  • Is there a bus stop?

There are (plural)

  • There are two bedrooms.
  • There aren't any shops.
  • Are there any chairs?

2En pasado

There is (singular / incontable)

  • There was a problem.

There are (plural)

  • There were a lot of people.

3Preposiciones de lugar

Preposición Significado Ejemplo
in dentro de The keys are in my bag.
on encima de (tocando) The book is on the table.
under debajo de The cat is under the bed.
next to al lado de The bank is next to the café.
between entre (dos) It's between the bank and the school.
opposite enfrente de The park is opposite my house.
in front of delante de There's a car in front of the door.
behind detrás de The garden is behind the house.
near cerca de I live near the beach.
above encima (sin tocar) There's a lamp above the table.

4At, in, on con lugares

at

un punto: at home, at work, at school, at the station

in

dentro, ciudades, países: in the kitchen, in Madrid, in Spain

on

superficies, calles, transporte: on the wall, on Oxford Street, on the bus

Ponlo en práctica

Los fallos se guardan para que los repases.
