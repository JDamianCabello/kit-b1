Errores típicos de hispanohablantes

Los fallos que más se repiten cuando pensamos en español. Repásalos antes de cada Writing.

Léelos en voz alta
en su forma correcta.
Incorrecto Correcto Por qué
I have 20 years. I'm 20 (years old). La edad va con be.
She don't like it. She doesn't like it. He / she / it → does.
Is raining. It's raining. El sujeto siempre aparece.
I live here since 2019. I've lived here since 2019. Sigue ahora: present perfect.
I have seen him yesterday. I saw him yesterday. Fecha terminada: past simple.
in next Friday next Friday Sin preposición con next / last / this.
at 6 of the afternoon at 6 in the afternoon / at 6 pm in the afternoon.
ago two years two years ago ago va detrás.
since two weeks for two weeks for + duración.
because of I like it because I like it because of + sustantivo.
Although it was cold, but we went out. Although it was cold, we went out. although y but no van juntos.
I went for buy bread. I went to buy bread. Finalidad: to + verbo.
The life is beautiful. Life is beautiful. En general, sin the.
people is people are people es plural.
an information / advices some information / some advice Incontables.
more bigger bigger No se mezclan more y -er.
I am agree. I agree. agree es un verbo.
Where you live? Where do you live? Las preguntas llevan auxiliar.
