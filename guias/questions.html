Cómo hacer preguntas

En inglés casi siempre hace falta un auxiliar delante del sujeto.

Where you live?
Where do you live?

1Forma

Con be

Be + sujeto + ...?

Con do / does / did

Do / Does / Did + sujeto + verbo?

Con otro auxiliar

can / will / have + sujeto + verbo?

2Ejemplos

Con be

  • Are you tired?
  • Where is the station?
  • Was it expensive?

Con do / does / did

  • Do you like pizza?
  • Where does she work?
  • What did you do yesterday?

Con otro auxiliar

  • Can you swim?
  • Will it rain tomorrow?
  • Have you finished?

3Palabras interrogativas

Inglés Español Ejemplo
What qué What's your name?
Where dónde Where do you live?
When cuándo When is your birthday?
Who quién Who is that man?
Why por qué Why are you sad? – Because...
How cómo How are you?
How old cuántos años How old are you?
How much cuánto (precio, incontables) How much is it?
How many cuántos (contables) How many brothers have you got?
How often con qué frecuencia How often do you go to the gym?
How long cuánto tiempo How long does it take?
Which cuál (entre opciones) Which do you prefer, tea or coffee?
Whose de quién Whose bag is this?
What time a qué hora What time does the film start?

4Respuestas cortas

Do you like it? – Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Is she Spanish? – Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

Queda raro contestar solo «Yes» o «No».

Ponlo en práctica

Los fallos se guardan para que los repases.
