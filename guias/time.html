Expresiones de tiempo

Las preposiciones de tiempo y las palabras for, since, ago, during y while.

in next Friday
next Friday

1Se usa con

in

  • meses
  • años
  • estaciones
  • partes del día
  • siglos

on

  • días
  • fechas
  • días + parte del día
  • días especiales

at

  • horas
  • night
  • the weekend (UK)
  • Christmas, Easter

2Ejemplos

in

in Julyin 2024in summerin the morningin the afternoon

on

on Mondayon 5th Mayon Friday morningon my birthdayon Christmas Day

at

at 9 o'clockat nightat midnightat the weekendat Christmas

3Sin preposición

Delante de next, last, this, every, tomorrow, yesterday no va in / on / at.

See you next Friday. I saw him last week.

4For, since, ago, during, while

Palabra Significado Ejemplo
for durante (una cantidad de tiempo) I've lived here for ten years.
since desde (un momento) I've lived here since 2015.
ago hace (va detrás) I moved here ten years ago.
during durante + sustantivo I fell asleep during the film.
while mientras + sujeto y verbo I fell asleep while I was watching the film.
by como muy tarde Send it by Friday.
until hasta I worked until 6 pm.
