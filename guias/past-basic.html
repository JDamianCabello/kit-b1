Past simple desde cero

Was / were, verbos regulares con -ed y cómo hacer negativas y preguntas.

I didn't went.
I didn't go.

1Forma

was / were

  • I / he / she / it was
  • you / we / they were
  • wasn't · weren't

Verbos regulares

  • verbo + -ed: work → worked
  • -e: live → lived
  • consonante + y: study → studied
  • CVC: stop → stopped

Negativas y preguntas

  • didn't + verbo base
  • Did + sujeto + verbo?
  • Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

2Ejemplos

was / were

  • I was at home yesterday.
  • They were very happy.

Verbos regulares

  • We watched a film.
  • She studied all night.

Negativas y preguntas

  • I didn't see him.
  • Did you like it?

3Cómo se pronuncia -ed

Sonido Cuándo Ejemplos
/t/ tras p, k, f, s, sh, ch worked, watched, stopped
/d/ tras sonidos sonoros (la mayoría) played, lived, cleaned
/ɪd/ (sílaba extra) solo tras t o d wanted, needed, decided

Los irregulares (go → went) están en la guía «Verbos irregulares».

Ponlo en práctica

Los fallos se guardan para que los repases.
