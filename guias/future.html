El futuro: will · going to · present continuous

Tres formas de hablar del futuro según lo decidido que esté.

Oferta rápida:
I'll help you!
I help you!

1Cuándo se usa

will

  • Decisión en el momento
  • Predicción u opinión
  • Ofrecimientos y promesas

going to

  • Plan o intención ya decidida
  • Predicción con pruebas (lo estás viendo)

present continuous

  • Cita o plan organizado, con día, hora o lugar

2Forma

will

will + verbo

✗ won't + verbo

going to

am/is/are going to + verbo

✗ isn't going to + verbo

present continuous

am/is/are + verbo-ing

+ día u hora

3Ejemplos

will

  • The phone's ringing. I'll get it.
  • I think Spain will win.
  • I won't tell anyone, I promise.

going to

  • I'm going to study medicine.
  • Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.

present continuous

  • I'm meeting Ana at 6 tomorrow.
  • We're flying to Rome on Friday.

5Primer condicional (muy frecuente en el B1)

If + present simple, will + verbo

If it rains, we'll stay at home.

Nunca «If it will rain».
