Past simple vs Past continuous

La acción corta interrumpe a la acción larga. Clave para contar historias en el Writing.

I was having a shower when the phone rang.

1Cuándo se usa

Past simple

  • Acción corta y terminada
  • Acciones una detrás de otra
  • La acción que interrumpe

Past continuous

  • Acción larga en progreso
  • El escenario de una historia
  • Dos acciones a la vez (while)

2Forma

Past simple

Sujeto + verbo-ed / irregular

I worked · I went · I didn't go

Past continuous

Sujeto + was/were + verbo-ing

I/he/she/it was · you/we/they were

3Ejemplos

Past simple

  • I got up, had breakfast and left.
  • The phone rang.
  • Suddenly, the lights went out.

Past continuous

  • At 8 pm we were watching TV.
  • The sun was shining and birds were singing.
  • While I was cooking, he was studying.

6Diferencias clave

Past simple Past continuous
Acciones terminadas Acciones en progreso
Acciones cortas Acciones largas
Una secuencia de hechos Información de fondo, el escenario
Hechos del pasado Una hora concreta del pasado
Interrumpe una acción Es interrumpida por una acción corta
