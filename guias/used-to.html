Used to

Hábitos y situaciones del pasado que ya no son así.

I used to have long hair.
(ahora no)

1Forma

Afirmativa

used to + verbo

Negativa

didn't use to + verbo

Pregunta

Did you use to + verbo?

2Ejemplos

Afirmativa

  • I used to play football.
  • We used to live in Seville.

Negativa

  • I didn't use to like fish.
  • She didn't use to wear glasses.

Pregunta

  • Did you use to have a pet?

En negativa y pregunta se escribe use to (sin d), igual que «didn't go».

Ponlo en práctica

Los fallos se guardan para que los repases.
