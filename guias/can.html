Can, can't y cómo hablar de gustos

Habilidades, permiso y lo que te gusta hacer.

I can to swim.
I can swim.

1Uso

can / can't

  • Habilidad: I can swim.
  • Permiso: Can I go out?
  • Pedir algo: Can you help me?

like + -ing

  • Gustos en general
  • like, love, enjoy, hate, don't mind + -ing

would like

  • Querer algo (educado)
  • I'd like = I would like

2Forma

can / can't

can + verbo (sin to)

She can drive. He can't cook.

Pasado: could / couldn't

like + -ing

verbo + -ing

I like cooking.

She doesn't like getting up early.

would like

would like + sustantivo / to + verbo

I'd like a coffee.

I'd like to travel.

Ponlo en práctica

Los fallos se guardan para que los repases.
