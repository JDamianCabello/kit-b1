Pronombres y posesivos

Yo, me, mi, mío: cuatro palabras distintas en inglés.

This is my book.
It's mine.

1La tabla completa

Sujeto Objeto Adjetivo posesivo Pronombre posesivo
I me my mine
you you your yours
he him his his
she her her hers
it it its –
we us our ours
they them their theirs

2Cómo se usan

Objeto

Detrás del verbo o preposición

Call me. I love her. Come with us.

Adjetivo posesivo

Delante de un sustantivo

my car, their house

Pronombre posesivo

Solo, sin sustantivo

The car is mine.

3El genitivo 's

  • Ana's car = el coche de Ana
  • my parents' house = la casa de mis padres (plural: solo apóstrofo)
  • the children's toys (plural irregular: 's)

Ojo: its = su (de una cosa). it's = it is.

Ponlo en práctica

Los fallos se guardan para que los repases.
