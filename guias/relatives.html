Relativos: who, which, that, where, whose

Para unir frases y dar más información sin repetir. Muy útiles en el Writing.

The man who lives
next door is a chef.

1Se usa con

who

  • personas

which

  • cosas y animales

where

  • lugares

2Ejemplos

who

  • She's the girl who won the prize.

which

  • This is the book which I told you about.

where

  • That's the café where we met.

3That y whose

that

personas o cosas (sustituye a who / which)

The film that we saw was great.

whose

cuyo, de quien

That's the man whose car was stolen.

Ponlo en práctica

Los fallos se guardan para que los repases.

Practicar relativos

Toca los ejemplos marcados con para oírlos.
