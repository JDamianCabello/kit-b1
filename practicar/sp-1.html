Speaking Part 1: preguntas personales

  1. What's your name?

  2. Where do you live?

  3. Who do you live with?

  4. Do you work or are you a student?

  5. What do you like doing in your free time?

  6. Tell us about your family.

  7. What did you do last weekend?

  8. What are you going to do next weekend?

  9. Do you like cooking? Why or why not?

  10. What's your favourite food?

  11. How do you usually travel to work or school?

  12. Tell us about your best friend.

  13. What kind of music do you like?

  14. Where did you go on your last holiday?

  15. Do you prefer the city or the countryside? Why?

  16. How often do you use the internet?

  17. What's the weather like in your town?

  18. Tell us about a film you enjoyed.

  19. Why are you learning English?

  20. What would you like to do in the future?
