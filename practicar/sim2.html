Reading · Part 3

Lee el texto y contesta las preguntas.

A day with the elephants

Tom Barker has worked at Westfield Zoo for ten years. He looks after the elephants, and he says every day is different. "People think I just feed the animals, but that's only a small part of my job," he explains. "I also check that they're healthy, clean their home and plan activities so they don't get bored."

Tom didn't plan to work with animals. He studied computer science at university and got a job in an office. "I was earning good money, but I felt I was wasting my life in front of a screen," he says. When he saw an advert for volunteers at the zoo, he decided to try it at weekends. A year later, he left his office job.

The hardest part, he says, is the weather. "In winter it can be really cold at six in the morning. But when the baby elephant runs to say hello, I forget about everything else."

1

What do people think about Tom's job?

«People think I just feed the animals».

2

Why did Tom leave his office job?

«I felt I was wasting my life». Ganaba buen dinero.

3

What does Tom find difficult?

«The hardest part ... is the weather ... cold at six in the morning».

Reading · Part 4

Elige la frase que va en cada hueco. Sobra una.

When Maya was fourteen, she started a cooking channel on the internet. 1 She filmed the videos in her parents' kitchen with an old phone.

At first, only her friends watched. But one day, a video of her grandmother's chocolate cake was shared by a famous food magazine. 2 Suddenly, she had thousands of followers.

Now Maya is nineteen and studies at a cookery school in Paris. 3 She says she wants to open a small restaurant one day.

  1. A Her first recipes were very simple dishes, like pasta and salads.
  2. B In just one week, it had more than a million views.
  3. C She still posts a new video every Sunday.
  4. D Her grandmother never learned to cook.

Hueco 1

Habla de sus primeros vídeos y cómo los grababa.

Hueco 2

«it» = el vídeo compartido. Después: «Suddenly, she had thousands of followers».

Hueco 3

Presente: lo que hace ahora. La D contradice el texto (la abuela tenía una receta).

Reading · Part 6

I've been learning the guitar 1 two years. My teacher is very patient and always helps me when I make mistakes. I practise every day, even 2 I'm tired. Next month I'm going 3 play in a concert for the first time. I'm a bit nervous, but my family 4 coming to watch me.

going to + verbo.

Listening · Part 1

Pulsa «Escuchar» y elige la respuesta. Puedes oír cada audio dos veces.

Este navegador no puede leer textos en voz alta. Abajo tienes la transcripción para leerla.

1

Where are they going to meet?

Transcripción

Woman: Let's meet outside the museum at eleven.

Man: It's going to rain. Why don't we meet in the café opposite?

Woman: Good idea. The one next to the bank?

Man: Yes, that's it.

«Why don't we meet in the café opposite? – Good idea».

2

How much did the woman pay for her jacket?

Transcripción

Man: I love your jacket! Was it expensive?

Woman: It was eighty pounds, but it was in the sale, so I only paid forty.

Man: That's a really good price.

«I only paid forty». 80 era el precio original.

3

What will the weather be like tomorrow?

Transcripción

Man: Is it going to be sunny for the picnic tomorrow?

Woman: The forecast says it'll be cloudy all morning, but it won't rain.

Man: Great. As long as it's dry, I'm happy.

«it'll be cloudy ... but it won't rain».

Writing · Part 2 (historia)

Escribe unas 100 palabras. Tu historia debe empezar con esta frase:

When I opened the door, I couldn't believe my eyes.

Write your story.
