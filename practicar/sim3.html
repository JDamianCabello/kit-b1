Reading · Part 1

Lee cada texto y elige la opción que dice lo mismo.

1

CAFÉ
Free Wi-Fi for customers. Ask at the counter for the password. Maximum 1 hour at busy times.

«Free Wi-Fi for customers». En horas punta se puede usar, pero como máximo una hora.

2

Mum, the dentist called. My appointment on Tuesday is now at 4.30, not 3.30. Can you still take me? Olivia

El día es el mismo; cambia la hora. «Can you still take me?»

3

GYM
Please clean the machines after use. Towels available at reception.

«clean the machines after use» = límpialas cuando termines. Las toallas las dan en recepción.

B

Reading · Part 4

Elige la frase que va en cada hueco. Sobra una.

Last summer, my friend Leo and I cycled from London to Paris. 1 We trained for three months before the trip, riding 50 kilometres every weekend.

The first day was the hardest. It rained all day and Leo had a flat tyre. 2 Luckily, a farmer stopped and helped us fix it.

We arrived in Paris after four days. 3 We were tired, but we were really proud of ourselves.

  1. A It was something we had wanted to do for years.
  2. B We didn't have the right tools to repair it.
  3. C We celebrated with a big dinner near the Eiffel Tower.
  4. D Paris is the most visited city in the world.

Hueco 1

Explica por qué hicieron el viaje antes de hablar del entrenamiento.

Hueco 2

«it» = the flat tyre. Por eso necesitaron ayuda.

Hueco 3

Qué hicieron al llegar. La D no tiene relación con su historia.

Reading · Part 6

Escribe UNA palabra en cada hueco.

My name is Pedro and I live 1 a small town near Valencia. I 2 been learning English for six months. I study 3 evening after work. My favourite part of the course is speaking, 4 I find grammar quite difficult.

in + ciudad o pueblo.

Present perfect continuous: I have been learning.

every evening = todas las tardes.

Contraste: but (o although).

Listening · Part 1

Pulsa «Escuchar» y elige la respuesta. Puedes oír cada audio dos veces.

Este navegador no puede leer textos en voz alta. Abajo tienes la transcripción para leerla.

1

What did the woman lose?

Transcripción

Woman: I can't find my phone anywhere!

Man: Is it in your bag?

Woman: No. I've got my keys and my wallet, but not my phone.

Man: Let me call it... Oh, listen, it's ringing under the sofa.

Tiene las llaves y la cartera: «but not my phone».

2

What will they do on Saturday?

Transcripción

Man: Shall we go to the beach on Saturday?

Woman: The forecast says it'll rain. How about the cinema?

Man: We went last week. Let's visit the new museum instead.

Woman: OK, good idea.

Descartan la playa (lluvia) y el cine (ya fueron): «Let's visit the new museum».

Listening · Part 3

Vas a oír a una profesora hablando de un curso. Completa las notas con una palabra, un número o una fecha. Puedes escucharlo dos veces.

Este navegador no puede leer textos en voz alta. Abajo tienes la transcripción para leerla.

Summer art course

Starts: Monday 1 July

Classes finish at: 2

Price: £3

Bring: an old 4

Teacher's surname: 5

«Monday the fourteenth of July».

«until half past twelve» = 12:30.

«one hundred and twenty pounds».

«bring an old shirt».

Lo deletrea: G-R-E-E-N.

Transcripción

Hello everyone, and welcome to the summer art course.

Classes start on Monday the fourteenth of July, and finish on Friday the first of August.

They're from ten o'clock until half past twelve every morning.

The course costs one hundred and twenty pounds, and that includes all the paint and paper.

Please bring an old shirt to protect your clothes.

Your teacher is called Anna Green. That's G, R, E, E, N.
