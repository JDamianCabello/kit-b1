Reading · Part 3

Lee el texto y contesta las preguntas.

My first job

When I was sixteen, I got a summer job in an ice-cream shop by the sea. I thought it would be easy and fun. After all, who doesn't like ice cream? But it was much harder than I expected.

The shop was always busy, especially in the afternoons, and there was a long queue of hot, impatient customers. I had to work fast and remember dozens of flavours. My arm hurt from serving the hard ice cream, and at the end of each day I was exhausted.

But the job taught me a lot. I learned to stay calm when people were rude, and I discovered that I was good at working in a team. I also saved enough money to buy my first laptop. Looking back, it was one of the best summers of my life.

1

What did the writer expect the job to be like?

«I thought it would be easy and fun».

2

What was difficult about the job?

«remember dozens of flavours». Los maleducados eran algunos clientes, no el jefe.

3

How does the writer feel about the job now?

«it was one of the best summers of my life».

Listening · Part 1

Pulsa «Escuchar» y elige la respuesta. Puedes oír cada audio dos veces.

Este navegador no puede leer textos en voz alta. Abajo tienes la transcripción para leerla.

1

Which train will the man take?

Transcripción

Man: Excuse me, when's the next train to Oxford?

Woman: There's one at ten fifteen, but it's always very full. The next one is at ten forty-five.

Man: I'll take the ten forty-five, then.

«I'll take the ten forty-five».

2

What does the woman want to drink?

Transcripción

Man: What can I get you? A coffee?

Woman: No, thanks. I've had too much coffee today.

Man: Tea? Orange juice?

Woman: Just a glass of water, please.

«Just a glass of water, please».

3

How is the boy going to get to school?

Transcripción

Woman: Are you cycling to school today?

Man: My bike's broken, and Dad can't drive me because he's working early.

Woman: Then you'll have to walk.

Man: OK, I'll leave now.

La bici está rota y su padre no puede llevarle: va andando.

Listening · Part 3

«next to the lake».

«five pounds an hour».

Transcripción

Welcome to Greenfield Park.

The park opens at eight in the morning and closes at sunset.

The café is next to the lake, and it serves hot food until three o'clock.

If you want to hire a bike, it costs five pounds an hour.

Dogs are welcome, but they must stay on a lead near the playground.

Writing · Part 2 (artículo)

Escribe unas 100 palabras.

Articles wanted!

What's your favourite way to relax? What do you do, and why do you enjoy it?

Write an article answering these questions. The best articles will appear in our magazine.

Autoevaluación: marca lo que cumples

Ver respuesta modelo

Time to relax

Do you ever feel stressed after a long week? I do, and my favourite way to relax is cooking.

On Sunday afternoons I put on some music, open a recipe book and cook something new. Last week, for example, I made a Thai curry for the first time.

I enjoy it because I forget about work and concentrate on the food. It's also creative, and at the end I can share a delicious meal with my family.

In my opinion, everyone should find a hobby that helps them relax. Why don't you try cooking?
