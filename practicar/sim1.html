SWIMMING POOL
Children under 12 must be with an adult at all times. Lifeguard on duty 9 am – 6 pm only.

«must be with an adult at all times» = siempre con un adulto. El aviso no dice que no puedan usar la piscina después de las 6.

Reading · Part 3

Lee el texto y contesta las preguntas.

Learning to surf at 35

When I was a child, I was afraid of the sea. My family often went to the beach, but I always stayed on the sand with a book. Last summer, when I turned thirty-five, my friend Marta invited me to join her on a surfing course in Cornwall. At first I said no, but she kept asking, so in the end I agreed.

The first day was terrible. The water was freezing and I spent more time under the waves than on top of them. I felt embarrassed because everyone else in the group seemed to learn so quickly. However, our instructor, Pete, was very patient. He told me that fear is normal and that the most important thing is to enjoy being in the water.

By the end of the week, I could stand up on the board for a few seconds. It wasn't much, but I was really proud of myself. Now I go surfing whenever I can, and I've even persuaded my brother to try it.

1

Why did Laura go on the surfing course?

«she kept asking, so in the end I agreed».

2

How did Laura feel on the first day?

«I felt embarrassed because everyone else ... seemed to learn so quickly».

3

What does Laura say about the end of the week?

«I was really proud of myself».

Reading · Part 4

Elige la frase que va en cada hueco. Sobra una.

Five years ago, the empty land behind our street was full of rubbish. Then a group of neighbours decided to turn it into a community garden. 1 They spent every weekend for three months clearing the land.

Now the garden has more than thirty vegetable beds. 2 Older people teach the younger ones how to grow tomatoes and beans, and the children help with the heavy work.

Every September there is a big party to celebrate the harvest. 3 The money goes to buying seeds and tools for the next year.

  1. A At first, it wasn't easy.
  2. B Families of all ages look after them together.
  3. C Everyone brings food and there is a small market.
  4. D The rubbish is collected every Monday morning.

Hueco 1

«it wasn't easy» se explica después: pasaron tres meses limpiando.

Hueco 2

«them» = the vegetable beds. Después habla de mayores y niños.

Hueco 3

«The money» viene de la «small market».

Listening · Part 1

Pulsa «Escuchar» y elige la respuesta. Como en el examen, puedes oír cada audio dos veces. Tu móvil lee la conversación con voz sintética.

Este navegador no puede leer textos en voz alta. Abajo tienes la transcripción para leerla.

1

What time does the film start?

Transcripción

Woman: Shall we meet at the cinema at seven?

Man: The film starts at half past seven, but I want to buy some popcorn first, so let's meet at a quarter past seven.

Woman: OK, see you then.

«The film starts at half past seven». 7:15 es cuando quedan.

2

What is the man going to buy?

Transcripción

Man: I'm going to the shop. Do we need any milk?

Woman: No, I bought some this morning. But we haven't got any bread.

Man: OK. And eggs?

Woman: There are six in the fridge, that's enough.

«we haven't got any bread». La leche y los huevos ya los tienen.

3

How will the woman get to the airport?

Transcripción

Man: Are you taking the train to the airport tomorrow?

Woman: I usually do, but there's a train strike. I thought about getting a taxi, but it's too expensive, so my brother is driving me.

«my brother is driving me» = en coche.
